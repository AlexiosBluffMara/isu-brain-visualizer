# Data and dependency notices

## MRI data

The prepared viewer packages derive from OpenNeuro ds004199 version 1.0.6.
The dataset description declares CC0. Attribution and provenance are retained:

Schuch F et al. An open presurgery MRI dataset of people with epilepsy and
focal cortical dysplasia type II. Scientific Data 10, 475 (2023).
https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-023-02386-7
https://doi.org/10.18112/openneuro.ds004199.v1.0.6

Dataset license declaration:
https://github.com/OpenNeuroDatasets/ds004199/blob/1.0.6/dataset_description.json

The published data is used for education and research. The expert lesion mask
is the answer source. The viewer is not a diagnostic medical device.

## Browser libraries

NiiVue and Three.js are loaded from their pinned CDN URLs in the viewer HTML.

- NiiVue: https://github.com/niivue/niivue (BSD-2-Clause).
- Three.js: https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js (MIT).

Their licenses continue to govern those components.
